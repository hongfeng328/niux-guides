How families usually decide

Multiple ages at home? LifeVac's interchangeable masks cover adults and children with one device; Dechoker asks you to buy the size for each age group. One specific child? A single correctly sized Dechoker can be the simpler purchase. Car, stroller, grandparents' house? A second unit matters more than which brand — most choking events happen where food is served, and a device in a drawer at home does nothing at a restaurant. LifeVac sells a dedicated Travel Kit for this.
